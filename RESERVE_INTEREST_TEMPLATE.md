# Conditional reserve interest - no deposit requested

Prepared 9 November 2026. No USD 10,000 minimum or funded-mainnet reserve is asserted as an SCF application prerequisite. Build funding pays eligible development; reserve capital is a separate need before real-value protection is sold. The voter concern about credible LP demand remains relevant even without a universal threshold.

## Proposed validation work

Interview at least three prospective capital providers during the pilot and publish a consented demand summary. Seek named, conditional letters where interest is genuine. Report actual amounts and conditions; no minimum total is invented. If there are no commitments, report zero and keep real-value sales closed. Interviews or nonbinding interest must not be labeled funded TVL.

We understand reserve providers can incur losses. This document requests no deposit into unaudited contracts and promises no yield, guaranteed payout or SCF award. A nonbinding letter does not obligate funding and is reported as such. Actual deposits, if any, will be recorded separately with network, asset, transaction and verified amount.

No letter has been signed by this work. Stellar Build Delhi support and team-minted CFUSD tokens are not external LP commitments.
